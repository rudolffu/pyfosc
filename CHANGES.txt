2.0.0-alpha (unreleased)
------------------------
- Wavelength, flux and telluric calibration no longer need IRAF/PyRAF:
  new modules pyfosc.specio, pyfosc.wavecal, pyfosc.fluxcal, pyfosc.telluric
  and pyfosc.postcal.PostExtraction.
- pyfosc.steps.{identlamp2m,reidentlamp2m,wavecal2m,telluric_base2m} rewritten
  on top of them; `pyfosc run` and pyfosc_run.sh use only Python steps.
- Lamp lines are identified by matching the packaged templates in database/;
  a matplotlib window is the manual fallback.
- Standard stars are recognised from the OBJECT keyword (--std-file/--star/--caldir
  to override). Outputs are skipped when present (--overwrite to redo).
- XLT Fe-Ar solutions are refitted on the BFOSC atlas lines
  (iraf_data/newlinelists/fear_bfosc_atlas.txt); magnitudes of standard stars are
  converted with the IRAF constant (3.68e-20 erg/cm2/s/Hz at mag 0); the telluric
  fit falls back to the airmass ratio unless it is significant.
- Telluric correction reworked: the template takes a local continuum per band
  (now also 8100-8400 A on XLT) and carries its error; scale and shift are fitted
  per band with error bars; the scales of all targets of a night are combined and
  given to faint targets; all usable standards are used (water interpolated in
  time); a frame mislabelled as a standard is rejected; Paschen/He II lines of the
  standard are removed from the template. New QA: telluric_template_<star>.pdf and
  telluric_summary.csv. The O2 airmass exponent was measured on three XLT nights
  and is consistent with 1 (default unchanged).
- Documentation rewritten: README plus docs/ (installation, tutorial, checking
  your reduction, calibration steps, files and headers, troubleshooting).
- IRAF-based scripts under src/ are kept as legacy.
